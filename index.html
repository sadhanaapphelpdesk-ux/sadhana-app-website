Privacy Policy for Sadhana

Last Updated: September 15, 2025

Welcome to Sadhana ("we," "us," or "our"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Types of Users

  • Guest Users: No sign-in required. All data remains on your device.
  • Free Login Users: Sign in with Firebase Authentication to sync purchases and data across devices.
  • Pro Users: Unlock premium features through a one-time purchase.

2. Information We Collect

Guest Users

No personal data is collected. All mantras, sessions, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Free Login Users

When you create an account or sign in using Firebase Authentication, we collect your email address (or other login credential) and an authentication token. This is used solely for authentication and to restore your purchases across devices.

Data From Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication – for account creation and login (Privacy Policy)
  • RevenueCat – to validate and manage purchases (Privacy Policy)

3. Use of Information & Contact

We use your information only to provide and improve the App's core functionality, such as tracking statistics and restoring purchases.

Account Deletion Request

Last Updated: August 26, 2025

🛑 This Action is Permanent

Deleting your account is irreversible. All associated data will be permanently removed.

You can permanently delete your SadhanaApp account and all associated data directly from within the application. This action is irreversible and cannot be undone.

Steps to Delete Your Account (In-App)

  1. Open the **SadhanaApp** on your device.
  2. Log in to the account you wish to delete.
  3. Navigate to the **"Profile"** tab.
  4. Tap the prominent **"Delete Account"** button.
  5. Confirm the deletion by entering your password/credentials.
  6. Confirm the deletion.

What Data is Deleted?

The following data will be **permanently removed**:

  • Your user account (email and authentication credentials).
  • Your personal chant statistics (counts, streaks, totals).
  • Your saved custom mantras and private journal entries.
